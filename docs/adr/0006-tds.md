# ADR 0006 — TDS: client deductions, PAN on invoices, and the payee threshold on expenses

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-10
- Supersedes / extends: ADR 0005 (invoicing v2 — `Client.tdsPercent`, `Payment.tdsAmount/tdsPercent`)

## Context

The agency is an Indian company whose financial year runs 1 April – 31 March (company timezone, see ADR 0005
numbering). Two TDS flows matter at year end:

1. **Receivable** — clients deduct TDS when paying our invoices; the owner needs the FY total (and per client) to
   claim the credit. Not every client deducts, and some deduct on some invoices only.
2. **On expenses** — once payments to one payee cross a threshold within the FY, the company must deduct TDS when
   paying that payee. The owner wants a warning while recording the expense, not a surprise at audit time.

Invoices also need the client's legal name and PAN printed in the bill-to block.

## Decisions

### Client
- `Client.businessName` (optional): legal / business name printed on invoices; `name` stays the display name
  everywhere else. `Client.pan` (optional): stored upper-cased, validated as `[A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z]`.
- The PDF bill-to block prints `businessName` (fallback `name`) and a `PAN: …` line after the GSTIN.

### Per-invoice "client deducts TDS"
- `Invoice.tdsApplicable Boolean @default(false)`. `createInvoice` accepts `tdsApplicable?`; when omitted it defaults
  to `client.tdsPercent != null`. Parts of a plan inherit the first part's choice; recurring occurrences and proforma
  conversions copy the template / proforma; credit notes are always `false`.
- The wizard shows the toggle on the client step (defaulting as above, with the client's % in the hint) and repeats it on
  the review step. `InvoiceDetail` exposes `tdsApplicable` and `client.tdsPercent`.
- Payment sheet: `tdsApplicable = true` → TDS % / amount pre-filled from the client card (as before);
  `false` → the fields start empty behind a "Client deducted TDS?" toggle.
- Recording a payment with `tdsAmount > 0` on an invoice marked `false` sets `tdsApplicable = true`
  (`payment-core.ts`) so the year-end total is right.

### TDS on expenses (payee threshold per financial year)
- `Expense.tdsApplied Boolean`, `Expense.tdsPercent Decimal?`, `Expense.tdsAmount Decimal @default(0)`.
  `amount` stays the **gross** bill; net paid = `amount − tdsAmount`. When `tdsApplied` and only a % is given the
  amount is computed as `round2(amount × % / 100)`; TDS may not exceed the bill.
- `CompanySettings.tdsThresholdAmount Decimal @default(20000)` — editable in Settings → TDS
  ("Per payee, per financial year (resets every 1 April)").
- `src/server/finance/tds.ts`:
  - `fyRange(date, tz) → { start, end, key }` (`[1 Apr, next 1 Apr)` in `tz`, key from `financialYearKey`).
  - `vendorKey(vendor)` = trimmed lower-case; payees are matched case-insensitively on `Expense.vendor`
    (Prisma `equals` + `mode: "insensitive"`).
  - `vendorFyTotal(vendor, date, excludeExpenseId?)` = Σ `Expense.amount` for that payee in that FY.
  - `tdsThresholdStatus(vendor, amount, date, excludeId?) → { paidSoFar, withThis, threshold, crossed: withThis ≥ threshold, alreadyCrossed: paidSoFar ≥ threshold, fyKey }`.
  - `vendorTdsSummary(now)` → per payee: paid, TDS deducted, crossed. `tdsSummary(at)` → `receivable`
    (Σ `Payment.tdsAmount` by `receivedAt` in the FY), `onExpenses` (Σ `Expense.tdsAmount` by `date`), `byClient`.
- Actions (`expenses.ts`): `vendorTdsStatus(vendor, amount, date)` (financeRead) backs the live, debounced banner in
  the expense form. `createExpense` / `updateExpense` return `{ id, tdsWarning }`: the expense is always saved; when
  the payee is at/over the threshold and `tdsApplied` is false, every admin gets a `TDS_THRESHOLD` notification
  ("TDS threshold crossed for <vendor>", href `/admin/expenses`) and the warning text is returned.
- Expense rows carry a "TDS ₹…" chip; the "TDS" zone pill opens the per-payee FY sheet; CSV export and the Sheets
  mirror carry the TDS columns.

### Finance sheet
- Tiles "TDS receivable (FY <key>)" and "TDS deducted on expenses (FY)", plus the "TDS by client (FY)" list, with a
  This FY / Previous FY toggle (`?fy=previous`).

## Consequences
- Migration `20261010100000_tds_pan_business_name` (additive, defaults only). No backfill: existing invoices stay
  `tdsApplicable = false` until a TDS payment is recorded against them.
- `expenseFieldsSchema` is now a transformed object (ZodPipe): extend it with `.and()`/wrapping rather than `.extend()`.
